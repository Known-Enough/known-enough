# Privacy and data handling

## Private answers and shared results

Known Enough and its permitted AI service process private answers when consent is given. Other participants receive shared facts and explicitly authorized disclosures. Outcomes and timing can still allow inference; this is not a guarantee against all disclosure.

Exception permission, disclosure permission, and final proposal approval are separate decisions. Approvals refer to an exact proposal. Chat cannot save answers, grant permission, or approve on a participant's behalf.

## Chat

Decision chat is visible to everyone in the decision. Private assistant is visible only to its owner. Moving private text into Decision chat requires a preview and explicit post.

Chat AI receives the sender's bounded recent conversation and shared options. It excludes other participants' messages, other private threads, and saved private answers. Messages remain available for up to 30 days, shortened by retention settings or decision deletion. Application deletion does not establish deletion by the AI provider.

## Limits and recovery

The preview supports finite-choice decisions and groups of up to 16 members. Unsupported or ambiguous conditions require clarification. Validation checks supported conditions; it does not establish unrestricted optimality. The application does not make bookings, purchases, or external arrangements.

When a save or model response is unconfirmed, use the displayed status-check controls before repeating it. Unknown requests are not automatically replayed.
